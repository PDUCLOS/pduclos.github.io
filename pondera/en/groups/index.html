For groups that also buy companies

You acquire companies. Acquire their best prices too.

Every connected entity becomes a point of comparison. Pondera identifies the entity that gets the best price and quantifies the gap for the others, item by item.

Request a demonstrationSee how it works

The group grows. So does the comparison.

With each acquisition, its ERP is read, its item references are matched to yours, and its list prices enter the comparison. Nothing is written to its systems.
