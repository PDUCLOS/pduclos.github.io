Roadmap

What is in preparation. Nothing listed here is available today.

In preparation, no committed date
  • In preparation, no committed date

    Multi-user containerised deployment

    An installation in containers, on the customer side, for several users at the same time.

  • In preparation, no committed date

    Sign-in with the company directory

    Sign in with the company account rather than with an account specific to the tool.

  • In preparation, no committed date

    Editable supplier record

    Payment terms, group buyer, contract and transport, gathered and editable on one record.

  • In preparation, no committed date

    Import file per ERP

    A file in the format of each ERP, with the safe lines sorted out. The tool will still never write to the ERP: a person imports the file.

  • In preparation, no committed date

    Transport costs

    Take the cost of transport into account when comparing prices.

  • In preparation, no committed date

    Intercompany rules

    Take flows between companies of the same group into account when comparing prices.

  • In preparation, no committed date

    Company branding

    Adapt the tool's appearance to the customer's visual identity.

  • In preparation, no committed dateOption

    Local assistant

    An assistant that runs on your side, with no remote model, and suggests matches that the buyer accepts or rejects.

The order of the list indicates neither a priority nor a schedule. An item is announced as available only once it is delivered and tested.

What the tool does not do

These points are not on the roadmap.

  • Write directly to the ERP.
  • Forecast prices: advice starts from an announced increase.
  • Send your data to a remote artificial intelligence.
  • Host your data at the publisher.
