No cookies, no trackers

This website sets no cookies. It uses no audience measurement, no advertising, no social media buttons and no content loaded from another website.

Fonts and images are served by the website itself.

The only local storage: the form entries

On the demonstration request page, your entries are kept in the storage of your browser tab (sessionStorage). This way, you lose nothing if sending fails.

  • Entry name: demande-demo;
  • Content: the form fields you have filled in;
  • Duration: until the request is sent successfully, or until the tab is closed;
  • Recipient: nobody. This storage stays in your browser.

Why there is no banner

The website sets no cookies and uses no measurement or advertising trackers: it therefore shows no banner.
