A

ABC class
Ranking of items or suppliers by their weight in spend: A for the most significant, C for the least.
Announced increase
The increase stated by the supplier, usually calculated on its price list without regard to what you buy.
Annualised impact
The effect of a price change expressed as an amount over one year of purchases.

B

Buying ahead of an increase
Purchase brought forward to build stock at the current price, before an announced increase takes effect.

C

Carriage-paid threshold
The order amount or quantity above which the supplier pays for transport.
Conditional saving
A saving that still depends on a condition, most often a volume commitment to be met.
Cost of ownership
Total cost of holding stock: tied-up cash, storage, insurance, losses and obsolescence.

D

Delivered price
The purchase price plus the costs needed to deliver the goods to the site, according to the chosen Incoterm.
Description
Descriptive label of an item. It often contains dimensions, a material or a brand.
Discount
A reduction granted on the list price, expressed as a percentage or an amount.

E

Entity
A company or site of a group that buys in its own name, often with its own ERP.
ERP
Enterprise resource planning system: the software that records items, orders, receipts and invoices.

H

Human decision
A choice made by a person when an automatic rule cannot settle a case, for example between two candidate items.

I

Incoterm
An international trade rule, published by the International Chamber of Commerce, that allocates costs, risks and formalities between seller and buyer.
Indexation formula
A contract clause that adjusts a price according to one or more indices.
Intercompany
Describes a purchase or sale flow between two companies of the same group.
Intra-group resale
Resale of goods by one entity of a group to another entity of the same group, usually with a margin.
Item
A product listed in a company's ERP, identified by its own item code.
Item matching
The link between a supplier reference and the corresponding item in the ERP.

L

Landed costs
Costs added to the purchase price to bring goods to the site: transport, customs, insurance, handling.
List price
The price shown in the supplier's price list, before any discount. Also called gross price or catalogue price.

M

Matrix price list
A price list laid out as a cross table, for example one price per item and per entity or country.

N

Net price
The price actually paid after discounts are deducted.

P

Packaging unit
The quantity contained in the supplier's sales unit: a box, a roll, a drum.
Pareto
An analysis that ranks items by decreasing weight in spend to isolate the small number that accounts for most of it.
Price list version
The state of a price list at a given date. Comparing two versions measures what changed.

Q

Quantity break
A quantity threshold above which the unit price changes.

R

Raw material index
A published index that tracks the price of a raw material and serves as a reference for adjusting a price.
Read-only
Access that allows data to be viewed but not changed.

S

Secured saving
A saving observed on purchases already made, which no longer depends on any condition.
Simple average
Average of price changes in which every item counts equally, whatever the volume purchased.
Special price
A price granted by a supplier as an exception, for a given customer, item or period.
Spread between sites
Difference in the price obtained for the same item between the entities or sites of a group.
Stock coverage
The period during which available stock covers expected consumption.
Supplier reference
The code under which the supplier identifies a product in its price list.

U

Unique key
A column, or combination of columns, that identifies each line of a price list without ambiguity.

V

VAT
Value added tax. A price may be stated excluding or including tax.
Volume commitment
A quantity a buyer commits to order over a period in exchange for a price or a discount.

W

Weighted increase
Average increase calculated by giving each item a weight proportional to the volumes actually purchased.
