Discounts and special prices

From list price to final price, every discount is visible.

Price lists tracked by family and by supplier, discounts by country and by entity, special prices checked. The engine tells you whether the supplier raised its list price or cut its discount: they are not the same negotiation. When the item's sales are known, Buyvera quantifies margin erosion at constant selling price.

FAMILIES

Family discount, item discount, special price: each level is dated, versioned and can be undone.
