VisionTrack — on-premise computer vision

Real-time computer vision pipeline on a PC with a consumer NVIDIA GPU: RF-DETR detection, ByteTrack tracking, distance from a fusion of geometry and Depth Anything V2 depth, counting lines, restricted zones and heatmap. Everything is configured in one YAML file; outputs are an annotated video and JSON Lines.

LyonFlow

End-to-end MLOps platform for Lyon mobility: 9 open data sources, Medallion architecture on PostgreSQL/PostGIS, 27 Airflow DAGs, quantile XGBoost (P10/P50/P90) one hour ahead, MLflow tracking, PSI drift, pgRouting routing and a dashboard for 3 personas. Deployed on a VPS; Kubernetes target rendered with kustomize.

Grounded ERP copilot (RAG + SQL + LLMOps)

A copilot for finance teams on Sage / EBP ERPs: it searches internal procedures, queries the ERP read-only and cites every statement. Open-weight LLM on a local GPU via Ollama, native or LangChain chain, two-judge LLMOps benchmark, Docker image and Kubernetes manifests.

RAGLangChainOllamaLLMOpsLLM-as-a-judgeFastAPI

Stripe — real-time fraud

Stripe-style unified data architecture: transactional PostgreSQL, Debezium CDC to Kafka, Redis feature store, MongoDB, Snowflake warehouse — with XGBoost, MLflow, Evidently drift, automatic retraining and an AWS target fully described in Terraform.

PostgreSQLDebeziumKafkaRedisMongoDBSnowflake
 Industrial ML · Signal · MLOps

Vibration-based predictive maintenance

Fault diagnosis on the 32 real bearings of the Paderborn test rig from 5 explainable vibration indicators, with a validation that never puts the same bearing in both training and test, and a quantified measurement of the gap between lab-made and real faults. The model is served by a FastAPI API, containerizable and deployable on Kubernetes.
