The problem, the answer, the proof

In an international industrial group, the quality of reference data (items, prices, customers) drives invoicing, margin and the customer relationship. Errors are costly and often invisible.

Mission: unify SAP extracts, make reference data reliable and give European teams management dashboards.

For recruiters · business value
  • Quantified impact: −40% processing time and roughly €100K of errors detected every month.
  • International setting (FR/EN), European Customer Care and Master Data teams.
  • Dual skill set put into practice: HVAC business knowledge + data tooling.
For tech teams · what the code proves
  • Multi-source SAP Power BI semantic model, DAX quality-control measures.
  • Automation of recurring processing with Power Query and VBA.
  • Anomaly detection rules on reference data.
