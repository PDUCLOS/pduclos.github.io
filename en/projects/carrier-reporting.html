Carrier: 3 management dashboards across 12 countries, still in use after I left

Data Analyst, 12 months at Carrier HVAC Europe: three Power BI solutions designed, built and put into production on an SAP S/4HANA scope covering 12 countries, including a measure of how reliable the delivery promise to customers is. Every field is qualified with the business and IT, in an international FR/EN setting.

// In 30 seconds

The problem, the answer, the proof

In an international industrial group, indicators whose source nobody can trace are not used to decide: each country exports its own SAP data, and the delivery date promised to the customer has to be verifiable.

Mission: consolidate SAP extracts from 12 countries, qualify every field and give European teams dashboards they can trust.

For recruiters · business value
  • Lasting result: three management solutions put into production, still in use after I left.
  • Customer promise measured: lead-time gaps modeled (factory lead time, acknowledgment, reschedules, delays) across 12 countries.
  • Trusted data: the origin of every field validated with the Europe market lead and the IT department.
For tech teams · what the code proves
  • Power Automate orchestration of 12 daily extracts consolidated into a single reference dataset (1.5 GB reduced to 800 MB per cycle, under the Power BI Pro dataset limit).
  • Qualification and lineage: origin and calculation method of every field of an ABAP extract; reduction at source and query folding.
  • Semantic model in TMDL, bilingual FR/EN documentation generated from the model; explicit calculated columns for auditability.
  • Automatic detection and categorization of item-creation errors (Pareto) for the master data team.
