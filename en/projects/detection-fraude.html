Card fraud: alert in real time, report every morning

Containerized pipeline that polls a payment stream every minute, scores each transaction with XGBoost through a FastAPI API, sends a Telegram alert above a business threshold and produces an automatic daily report.

The problem, the answer, the proof

Card fraud exceeds one billion euros per year in Europe (ECB, 2019). Fraud teams need three things: to be notified instantly, to get a summary of the previous day every morning, and a prediction served continuously by an API.

The project addresses all three needs with infrastructure reproducible in two commands.

For recruiters · business value
  • Direct translation of a business need into an automated service: alert, report, API.
  • A deliberate trade-off: favor recall (don't let fraud through), with a threshold the business team can adjust without redeployment.
  • Willingness to challenge my own work: V1 was abandoned after a critical review (features not reproducible in production).
For tech teams · what the code proves
  • Airflow: real-time DAG (*/1 * * * *) with XCom and retries, daily report DAG at 07:00.
  • FastAPI: /predict, /health, optional API key, business threshold via environment variable, visible fallback to a backup scorer.
  • 10 business features reproducible at inference (amount, hour, weekend, merchant geolocation…), weighted XGBoost (scale_pos_weight ≈ 27).
  • Docker Compose with 6 services and health checks; PostgreSQL 15 via SQLAlchemy 2; Telegram / SMTP alerts.

Step by step

  1. Every minute, Airflow fetches current transactions (public API, falling back to a Kaggle file with a persistent cursor).
  2. Each transaction is sent to the FastAPI API, which builds the 10 business features and computes the fraud probability.
  3. Above the business threshold (0.7), an alert is sent on Telegram (and optionally by email); all results are stored in PostgreSQL.
  4. At 07:00, a second DAG generates the HTML report of the previous day's frauds.
  5. A Streamlit dashboard shows KPIs, probability distribution, a map and the top 20 riskiest transactions.

// Critical review

Known limitations and next steps

Documenting limitations honestly is part of the job: it lets a team make informed decisions.

  • Moderate precision (0.37 at threshold 0.5): probability calibration (CalibratedClassifierCV) and training on the full 555k rows are planned.
  • No CI or drift monitoring yet (Grafana + monthly retraining DAG planned).
  • The model is not versioned in the repository: a fresh clone serves the backup scorer until training has been run.
