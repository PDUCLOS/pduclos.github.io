Data Governance · Consulting · RNCP 38777 Block 1

Data governance: from assessment to action plan

Consulting engagement for an executive committee (Spotify case study, 450M users): maturity assessment across 9 dimensions, governance org chart, policy, rollout plan, budget and executive presentation. Later complemented by an executable demonstrator of the policy (Python, DuckDB SQL) and a quantified audit of the deliverables.

// In 30 seconds

The problem, the answer, the proof

Siloed data (marketing, product, content, engineering) and GDPR / CCPA / PDPA exposure with fines of up to 4% of global revenue: the company needs to structure its governance.

Educational case study based on a provided business case: the figures come from the case, not from a real engagement.

The file submitted to the jury (6 files for 4 stages) contained no code. It was later complemented, without changing the deliverables, by a demonstrator that runs the policy rules on synthetic data and an automated audit that recounts the figures in the deliverables.

For recruiters · business value
  • Advisory role with senior leadership: assessment, recommendation, budget, decision requested.
  • Targeted 6-month pilot on one domain (marketing engagement data) before scaling: a pragmatic approach.
  • Clear roles: DPO, CDO, data stewards, governance committee.
  • Candid about its own deliverables: the audit flags 5 points to review, reported without touching the jury version.
For tech teams · what the code proves
  • Target architecture: Databricks lakehouse + Unity Catalog as a common foundation.
  • Tooling recommended per need: catalog (Collibra), quality (dbt, Talend), compliance (OneTrust), security (SIEM), ML lifecycle (MLflow + Evidently).
  • Measurable governance KPIs: −10% missing data, 100% of consents tracked, incidents detected within 24 h.
  • Demonstrator: 21 SQL checks on DuckDB (quality, GDPR / CCPA, catalog), remediation then re-check with the same rules, 40 pytest tests.

// Deliverables and additions

What was submitted to the jury, what was added

The file submitted to the jury contained no code. To make it verifiable without inventing a service to deploy, two tools were added afterwards on a dedicated branch. The six jury deliverables were not modified: the discrepancies found are reported, not corrected.

Jury deliverables (unchanged)
  • Maturity assessment across 9 dimensions, scored 1 to 5.
  • Org chart and rationale note: sponsor, CDO, committee, DPO, CISO, legal, 4 data stewards.
  • Policy: 9 principles, 4 classification levels, 7 KPIs; implementation plan; 14-slide presentation.
Added · demonstrator
  • python -m gouvernance: 21 checks (11 quality, 6 GDPR / CCPA compliance, 4 catalog) in SQL on DuckDB.
  • 5,000 users and 50,148 synthetic events, defects injected at known rates (seed 38777).
  • 12 checks passing before remediation, 18 after; 40 pytest tests green.
Added · deliverables audit
  • audit_livrables.py reads the actual Word and PowerPoint files and recomputes maturity, budget and counts.
  • 12 points: 7 consistent, 5 flagged (3 numeric discrepancies, 1 untraceable, 1 to align).

// Results

What the audit and remediation found

Discrepancies found in the deliverables (not corrected)
  • Maturity: 2.33 in the assessment document (“about 2.3”), 2.4 on the slide 3 chart (22 / 9 = 2.44).
  • Pilot budget: the line “integration +30–50% of licences” is 80–130 K$, i.e. 30% of both bounds. Applied as written: 351–645 K$ instead of 350–560 K$ (upper bound underestimated by 85 K$).
  • “14 compliance controls” (slide 5): no matching list or matrix in the repository.
  • Timeline: plan ends at M18, policy at M24; switch to the Center of Excellence in phase 2 (months 7–12) in the policy, at M12 in the slides.
What remediation did (and refuses to paper over)
  • 147 countries filled in from subscriptions, 144 duplicates removed, 764 events quarantined, 478 events purged (> 24 months).
  • 65 consents without proof treated as absent, 55 audience rows removed for lack of consent, 29 plain-text e-mails pseudonymized, 1 non-catalogued column dropped.
  • Deliberately not fixed: missing birth year (to be re-collected), missing lineage, late DSARs and breaches notified late — these are process defects, not data defects.

Why neither Docker nor Kubernetes

The deliverable is a consulting file, and the demonstrator is a two-second batch job with no service, no state and no concurrent users. A container would add no further evidence. Measured scale-up: 50,000 users and 501,513 events, checks in 1.36 s, remediation and re-check in 1.37 s.

Step by step

  1. Maturity assessment across 9 dimensions: governance, compliance and integration at the “defined” level according to the assessment document (average ≈ 2.3 / 5).
  2. Organization design: centralized steering during the pilot, then transition to a Center of Excellence.
  3. Implementation plan: 6-month pilot on marketing engagement data, then 3 waves (M7–M18), compliance first.
  4. 14-slide executive presentation with budget, expected value and the decision requested from the committee.
  5. Added later — demonstrator: synthetic data generation, 21 checks, remediation, re-check and before / after report.
  6. Added later — deliverables audit: 12 points recomputed on the actual files, 7 consistent and 5 flagged.

// Critical review

Known limitations and next steps

Documenting limitations honestly is part of the job: it lets a team make informed decisions.

  • Certification case study: recommendations (organization, tools, budget) not implemented; vendor prices taken from the sources cited in the plan, not re-checked.
  • Demonstrator on synthetic data; target tools simulated (Databricks by DuckDB, dbt / Talend by SQL queries, Collibra by catalog.toml), none installed or tested.
  • The “quality score” is an average of pass rates: it stays at 98% while the “birth year” check is at 91.7%.
  • Discrepancies between the presentation and the assessment document (2.4 vs 2.3), and in the pilot budget: reported and not corrected in the jury deliverables.
  • “ROI > 3×” stated in the presentation, with no calculation in the repository.
  • No Docker or Kubernetes, deliberately: there is no service to deploy. GitHub Pages showcase not verified online (branch not merged).
