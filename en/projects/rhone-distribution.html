Snowflake and dbt: reliable data, regulated access

A fictitious B2B distributor in Lyon (5 branches, about 1,200 business customers) wants to track revenue, margin, DSO and service level, without everyone seeing everything. The Snowflake flow loads aggregated data every night, dbt turns it into tested Silver and Gold layers, and access is regulated by role: branch, personal data, costs. Built with dbt Core and validated end to end locally; Snowflake scripts ready, not yet run on a Snowflake account.

The problem, the answer, the proof

An industrial supplies distributor (PPE, tools, fasteners, plumbing, electrical) wants reliable reporting: finance tracks revenue, margin, DSO and aged balance, sales management tracks performance by branch and product family, and each sales rep sees only their own customers, in their own branch, without purchase costs.

Data: Rhône Distribution is a fictitious company and its ERP is simulated by a reproducible generator. The customer profile (NAF sectors, sizes, towns) comes from a real aggregate of 7,210 SIRENE establishments in the Rhône, Ain, Isère and Loire areas. No real company name or SIREN number is attached to a simulated history.

For recruiters · business value
  • Quality proven, not claimed: 70 injected anomalies, and a test that requires finding all of them, no more, no less.
  • Governance in the model: who sees what is declared in the dbt YAML and applied automatically.
  • Business names: Gold dimensions and tables named in French, ready for Power BI.
  • An honest status: what runs locally, and what still has to run on Snowflake.
For tech teams · what the code proves
  • Simulated ERP in PostgreSQL, nightly extract aggregated to day × customer × product × branch.
  • Bronze (COPY INTO on Snowflake, DuckDB locally) → Silver and Gold modelled with dbt.
  • Incremental fact table fct_ventes_jour, dimensions, finance (aged balance, DSO) and sales aggregates.
  • Row access policy per branch, masking policies on personal and financial data, classification tags.
  • Idempotent Snowflake load, stored procedure and scheduled Task, dbt documentation with lineage graph.

// Data quality

A ground truth: 70 anomalies, 70 found

Injected anomalyCount

The generator injects 70 anomalies and writes their exact count to a manifest. The Silver layer quarantines them, and a dbt test requires the quarantine to match that manifest exactly, no more, no less. The test has already paid off: a first "zero price" rule produced 5 false positives (a sale cancelled by a same-day return). The build failed and the rule was fixed.

Step by step

  1. Generate — reproducible simulated ERP (Parquet) and manifest of injected anomalies.
  2. Load — PostgreSQL ERP database, then nightly aggregated extract.
  3. Transform — Bronze, then Silver and Gold with dbt: 64 tests, including the anomaly ground truth.
  4. Secure — roles, row access and masking applied by the dbt post-hook.
  5. Expose — Gold tables ready for Power BI, feature snapshot for a churn model.
