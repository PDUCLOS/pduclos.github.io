// Frequent objections

The questions you may be asking yourself

“It's a career change: is he really at the level?”

Two RNCP certifications earned (RNCP is the French national qualification register): level 6, then level 7 (Master's level) Architecte en intelligence artificielle (AI Architect) in 2026, plus a Databricks certification, 10 months at Carrier with quantified results, and an MLOps platform deployed in production. The career change is complete; what remains is 20 years of field experience that junior profiles don't have.

“Senior on the business side, but junior on the tech side?”

The code is public and verifiable: around 1,600 automated tests in total, continuous integration, Docker, Airflow, MLflow, Terraform. The tech profile page details each proof.

“Why not a younger, less expensive profile?”

Because the cost of a failed data project almost never comes from the code: it comes from a badly framed problem. I know how to talk to a sales director, a buyer, a maintenance technician — and translate their need into KPIs.

“Will he fit into a tech team?”

My projects follow team standards: documented conventions, blocking tests in CI, architecture documentation, draw.io diagrams. Experience with team projects during training and in an international setting at Carrier.

“What kind of role?”

Analytics Engineer or Data Project Manager in industry, energy, B2B distribution or supply chain — where combined business and data expertise brings the most value. Lyon area, hybrid.
