// Background

From industrial distribution to Data

After 14 years at Michaud Chailly (Maurin Group) and 6 years within the Descours & Cabaud group (DEXIS, Codima), I developed in-depth knowledge of industrial B2B markets, supply chain and customer relationships.

In 2024, I started a career change into Data Analytics through the M2i / Jedha Bootcamp certified program (RNCP level 6, Bachelor's level), convinced that my business understanding is a decisive asset for making sense of data.

My time at Carrier (2-month internship + 8-month fixed-term contract, international FR/EN environment) allowed me to apply my new skills to real HVAC challenges: Power BI reporting, SAP analysis and process automation.

Certified in Databricks Lakehouse Fundamentals (2025), I keep broadening my technical foundation to address modern data challenges.
